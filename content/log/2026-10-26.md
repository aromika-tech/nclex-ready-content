## What was learned this run

- jsDelivr refuses new files: the repo is over its 50 MB package limit. Media now comes from raw.githubusercontent.com pinned to the commit; Buffer reads the mp4 as video/mp4 and the jpgs as image/jpeg.
- Video thumbnails: Buffer rejects thumbnailUrl ("social networks do not accept custom video thumbnail images"). thumbnailOffset works on Instagram only and was kept at 300 ms (the cover). Facebook and YouTube take their own frame; the cover is the opening frame.
- First comments need a paid Buffer plan. The question went into the caption instead.
- Instagram stickerFields.text was accepted on all three IG Stories. Whether Instagram shows it needs checking in the app.
- The block look breaks in video (heading colours vanish on cream). build_video.py now falls back to notecard.
- Interactive features tried: comment questions in every caption, sticker text on IG Stories, link stickers.
