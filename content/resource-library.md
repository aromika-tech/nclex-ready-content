# Verified Free Study Resources: Australian NCLEX-RN Preparation

All links checked live in August 2026. **Free** unless marked otherwise. These are the sources the 70-topic curriculum cites, so every post you publish can point at a primary source rather than an opinion.

## The exam itself

- **2026 NCLEX-RN Test Plan** (effective April 2026, runs to March 2029), the official blueprint, free PDF. This edition renamed "Safety and Infection Control" to **Safety and Infection Prevention and Control**, and replaced "substance abuse" with "substance misuse". <https://www.nclex.com/test-plans.page>
- **NCSBN Next Generation NCLEX resources**: free sample case-study pack and candidate tutorial. <https://www.nclex.com/next-generation-nclex.page>
- **Clinical Judgment Measurement Model**: the six measurable cognitive skills the case studies are built on. <https://www.nclex.com/clinical-judgment-measurement-model.page>

## Nursing regulation in Australia

- **Registered Nurse Standards for Practice**: NMBA. The seven standards. <https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/codes-guidelines-statements/professional-standards/registered-nurse-standards-for-practice.aspx>
- **Enrolled Nurse Standards for Practice**: NMBA. Essential for delegation questions, because the Australian skill mix differs from the US. <https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/Codes-Guidelines-Statements/Professional-standards/enrolled-nurse-standards-for-practice.aspx>
- **Code of Conduct for Nurses**: NMBA. Ethics, boundaries, cultural safety. <https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/codes-guidelines-statements/code-of-conduct.aspx>
- **Decision-Making Framework**: NMBA. The structured scope-of-practice questions used here instead of a US-style task list. <https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/codes-guidelines-statements/frameworks.aspx>
- **Mandatory notifications**: AHPRA. An obligation with no direct US equivalent. <https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Notifications/mandatorynotifications.aspx>
- **Joint statement on culturally safe care**: NMBA and CATSINaM. <https://www.nursingmidwiferyboard.gov.au/codes-guidelines-statements/position-statements.aspx>

## Safety and quality (ACSQHC)

- **NSQHS Standards**: the eight national standards every Australian hospital is accredited against. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/nsqhs-standards>
- **Clinical Care Standards library**: sepsis, acute coronary syndromes, delirium, hip fracture, VTE prevention, antimicrobial stewardship, stroke. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/clinical-care-standards>
- **Sepsis Clinical Care Standard**. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/clinical-care-standards/sepsis-clinical-care-standard>
- **Recognising and Responding to Acute Deterioration Standard**: underpins MET call criteria. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/nsqhs-standards/recognising-and-responding-acute-deterioration-standard>
- **Comprehensive Care Standard**: falls and pressure injury screening. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/standards/nsqhs-standards/comprehensive-care-standard>
- **High risk medicines (APINCHS)**. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/our-work/medicines-safety-and-quality/high-risk-medicines>
- **Australian Open Disclosure Framework**. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/our-work/open-disclosure/the-open-disclosure-framework>
- **ISBAR resources**: mandated under Standard 6. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/publications-and-resources/resource-library/isbar-poster>
- **Antimicrobial stewardship and AURA surveillance**. <https://www.safetyandquality.gov.au/our-work/antimicrobial-stewardship>

## Resuscitation and emergency

- **ANZCOR Guidelines**: the Australian and New Zealand resuscitation algorithms. Note these differ from the AHA algorithms in most US material. <https://www.anzcor.org/>
- **ANZCOR Guideline 9.2.7. Anaphylaxis**: adrenaline, not epinephrine. <https://www.anzcor.org/home/first-aid-management-of-medical-conditions/guideline-9-2-7-first-aid-management-of-anaphylaxis/>

## Infection prevention

- **Australian Guidelines for the Prevention and Control of Infection in Healthcare**: NHMRC, full free PDF. <https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/about-us/publications/australian-guidelines-prevention-and-control-infection-healthcare-2019>
- **5 Moments for Hand Hygiene**: Hand Hygiene Australia, with free eLearning. <https://hha.org.au/hand-hygiene/5-moments-for-hand-hygiene>

## Medicines

- **Australian Prescriber**: free, peer-reviewed, continually updated. The single best free Australian medicines resource. <https://australianprescriber.tg.org.au>
- **TGA**: registration, safety alerts, product information. <https://www.tga.gov.au>
- **NSW Clinical Excellence Commission, high-risk medicines**, including the intravenous potassium protocol. <https://www.cec.health.nsw.gov.au/keep-patients-safe/medication-safety/high-risk-medicines>
- **ANZSBT**: blood product administration guidelines (Australian rather than US AABB standards). <https://anzsbt.org.au/guidelines/>
- *Paid, noted for completeness:* Therapeutic Guidelines (eTG), Australian Medicines Handbook, Australian Injectable Drugs Handbook.

## Laboratory reference intervals

- **RCPA Manual, harmonised reference intervals**: the authoritative Australian adult ranges **in SI units**. This is the single most valuable resource for a nurse who trained on US material, because Australia reports in mmol/L, micromol/L and g/L rather than mg/dL and g/dL. <https://www.rcpa.edu.au/Manuals/RCPA-Manual>

## Condition-specific Australian guidance

- **Stroke Foundation. Living Clinical Guidelines** (continuously updated). <https://informme.org.au/guidelines/living-clinical-guidelines-for-stroke-management>
- **Heart Foundation, clinical guidelines**. <https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/for-professionals>
- **Kidney Health Australia. CKD Management Handbook** and the sick-day rules. <https://kidney.org.au/health-professionals/ckd-management-handbook>
- **Australian Asthma Handbook**: National Asthma Council. <https://www.asthmahandbook.org.au/>
- **Australian Diabetes Society**. <https://www.diabetessociety.com.au/>
- **Australian Immunisation Handbook** and the **National Immunisation Program Schedule**: use these, never the US CDC schedule. <https://immunisationhandbook.health.gov.au/> · <https://www.health.gov.au/topics/immunisation/when-to-get-vaccinated/national-immunisation-program-schedule>
- **National cancer screening programs**: cervical (HPV-based, from 25, five-yearly), breast (50–74, two-yearly), bowel (from 45, two-yearly). All differ materially from US intervals. <https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/national-cervical-screening-program>
- **NHMRC alcohol guidelines**: an Australian standard drink is 10 g, not the US 14 g. <https://www.nhmrc.gov.au/health-advice/alcohol>
- **CareSearch**: palliative care knowledge network. <https://www.caresearch.com.au/>

## State and hospital guidelines (free and high quality)

- **Queensland Health clinical guidelines**: maternity and neonatal guidance used nationally. <https://www.health.qld.gov.au/clinical-practice/guidelines-procedures>
- **NSW Clinical Excellence Commission**: Between the Flags, SEPSIS KILLS, medication safety. <https://www.cec.health.nsw.gov.au/>
- **Royal Children's Hospital Melbourne. Clinical Practice Guidelines**. <https://www.rch.org.au/clinicalguide/>
